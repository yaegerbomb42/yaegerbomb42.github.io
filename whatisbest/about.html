About

Last updated: 1 October 2026

WhatIsBest exists because buying the wrong thing is expensive and reading ten reviews that all say "it depends" is worse than useless. We test products ourselves, score them on numbers we measured, and publish one clear winner per category.

What we actually do

1 · Buy retail

We purchase every product with our own money at retail price. No review units, no special treatment, no preview access.

2 · Test for weeks

Each product goes through a minimum 30-day hands-on protocol with instrumented measurements rather than first impressions.

3 · Score on data

Weighted scorecards across 6-10 criteria per category. Every score traces back to a number we measured and can show you.

4 · Re-test quarterly

Prices change, updates ship, quality slips. We re-verify rankings every 90 days and demote products that regress.

How we score

Each category has its own weighted criteria, published on the page itself so you can re-weight them for your own priorities. A VPN is scored differently from a mattress because speed, privacy and value mean different things in each. The weighting is always visible — we do not hide the methodology behind a proprietary "score".

If budget matters more to you than raw performance, our tables are sortable by value score, and the right pick for you might be our number three. Publishing the full breakdown is deliberate: it lets you disagree with us using the same data we used.

How we make money

Affiliate commissions and advertising. When you click a "Check price" link and buy something, the retailer pays us a small percentage. It costs you nothing — prices are identical whether you use our link or not. We also display ads supplied by third-party networks.

Two structural protections keep this from corrupting the rankings. First, we buy everything ourselves, so we are never grateful to a vendor. Second, commission rates across the top products in a category are broadly similar, so there is no financial reason to rank our number two above our number one. Full detail is in our Affiliate Disclosure.

What we will not do

  • Accept payment for placement, scores or reviews.
  • Publish a ranking we have not tested ourselves.
  • Invent testing hours, sample sizes or scores. Every number on a page came from work we did.
  • Recommend a free product as "best" when a paid one measured materially better.

Corrections

We make mistakes and we fix them. If a figure looks wrong, a price has changed, or a product has been discontinued, tell us via the contact page and we will verify and update the page. Corrections are applied to the page and reflected in its "last verified" date — we do not quietly rewrite history.

Also on this site

Alongside the product rankings we run a community comparison tool where you can vote head-to-head on anything from pizza toppings to programming languages. It is separate from the affiliate rankings and carries no commercial links.
