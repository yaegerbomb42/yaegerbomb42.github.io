Affiliate Disclosure

WhatIsBest.cloud is reader-supported. When you purchase through links on this site, we may earn an affiliate commission from the retailer or brand — at no additional cost to you. Prices are identical whether you use our links or not.

How we make money

We participate in affiliate programs including (but not limited to) Amazon Associates, Impact, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, and direct partner programs with products we review. Links that may earn a commission are tagged rel="sponsored nofollow" in line with FTC guidance.

Why this doesn't corrupt our rankings

  • We buy every test product at retail with our own money — no review units.
  • Scores are locked before commerce links are added to a page.
  • Commission rates across top products in a category are broadly similar, removing any incentive to rank #2 above #1.
  • Brands cannot pay for placement, reviews, or score changes. Ever.
