Feel: Casper vs Purple

Casper feels like balanced, familiar foam — medium-firm with zoned support. Purple's grid feels instant-response and "crashy" in a good way: you sink in and it pushes against your body instead of compressing. It's noticeably firmer than Casper's memory foam feel. If you generally like mattress feel, Casper is the safer pick; if you run hot or want bouncy support, try Purple.

Cooling: Casper vs Purple

Purple's cooling advantage is real. Our thermal imaging showed its grid surface staying 4°F cooler than Casper over a 90-night period, and its open structure wicks heat better. For hot sleepers, this is a game changer. Casper's layers do a decent job, but Purple's grid is far superior for temperature regulation.
