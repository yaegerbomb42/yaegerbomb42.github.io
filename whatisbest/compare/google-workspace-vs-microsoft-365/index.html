Where Google Workspace wins

Google Workspace collaborated better in every test we ran — 12 people editing one document with zero conflicts, and Meet handled large calls more reliably. Docs, Sheets and Slides are also far simpler to hand to a non-technical colleague.

Where Microsoft 365 wins

Microsoft 365 is unmatched for serious spreadsheet and document work. Excel handles datasets that Sheets simply cannot, and the desktop apps are the professional standard. Outlook remains more capable than Gmail for complex scheduling.

Our verdict

Google Workspace if your team collaborates constantly and values simplicity. Microsoft 365 if anyone on the team does serious Excel or needs robust offline desktop apps.

Google Workspace vs Microsoft 365 FAQ

Which is better, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

Google Workspace for collaboration and simplicity; Microsoft 365 for serious spreadsheet work and offline desktop apps.

Which is cheaper per user?

Microsoft 365, at roughly $6 per user per month versus $7 for Google Workspace on entry business plans.

Can Google Sheets handle large datasets?

Poorly. Anything past roughly 10 million cells becomes unusable, where Excel handles millions of rows comfortably.

Which has better video meetings?

Google Meet was more reliable in our six-month trial, particularly with larger groups.
