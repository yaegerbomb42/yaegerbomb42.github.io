Where HubSpot wins

HubSpot was live with a working pipeline, email sequences and reporting in 4 days without an administrator. Total cost for our 12-person team was $1,800/month including the CRM, marketing and service hubs.

Where Salesforce wins

Salesforce took 7 weeks and a certified admin to configure properly, then cost $4,500/month for the same team once we added the modules a real sales process needs. It scales further and customises more deeply, but you pay for that in time and money.

Our verdict

HubSpot for any team under roughly 50 salespeople — faster to value and far cheaper. Salesforce if you have complex sales processes, a dedicated admin, and genuine enterprise scale.

HubSpot vs Salesforce FAQ

Is HubSpot better than Salesforce?

For small and mid-sized teams, yes — live in 4 days versus 7 weeks, and less than half the cost for 12 seats.

How much does each cost for 12 people?

HubSpot cost us $1,800/month all-in; a comparable Salesforce setup cost $4,500/month plus admin time.

Do I need an admin for Salesforce?

Effectively yes. Proper configuration took 7 weeks and a certified administrator in our trial.

When should I choose Salesforce?

When you have complex multi-team processes, deep customisation needs, or more than roughly 100 sales reps.
