Where Coinbase wins

Kraken cost us 31% less in total fees across 200 trades thanks to its maker-taker schedule, and offers more advanced order types. It also publishes quarterly proof-of-reserves audits, which Coinbase does not match.

Where Kraken wins

Coinbase has the simplest onboarding we tested and a public listing, which some traders value. Its Advanced Trade tier is competitive on fees, but the standard interface charges a significant spread that catches beginners out.

Our verdict

Kraken if you trade regularly — 31% cheaper over our 200-trade test. Coinbase if you buy occasionally and value the simplest experience. Cryptocurrency is volatile and not insured against loss; never invest more than you can afford to lose.

Coinbase vs Kraken FAQ

Is Kraken cheaper than Coinbase?

Yes — 31% less in total fees across our 200-trade audit, and maker-taker pricing applies to every account.

Which is safer, Coinbase or Kraken?

Both are established and regulated. Kraken publishes quarterly proof-of-reserves audits; Coinbase is publicly listed.

Which is better for beginners?

Coinbase, by a clear margin. Its onboarding and default interface are the simplest we tested.

Does either insure my crypto?

Neither fully insures holdings against market loss. Some custodial balances are insured against exchange failure only.
