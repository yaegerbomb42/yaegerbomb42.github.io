Where HelloFresh wins

HelloFresh averaged 34 minutes from box to table and never repeated a recipe in eight weeks. Portions were generous and the recipe cards were genuinely clear. It is the better choice for weeknight cooking.

Where Blue Apron wins

Blue Apron averaged 41 minutes and repeated three recipes, but its meals taught more technique — proper butchery, sauce work, seasoning. If cooking skill is the goal rather than dinner, Blue Apron is more educational.

Our verdict

HelloFresh for regular weeknight dinners — faster, more varied, easier to pause. Blue Apron if you want to actually learn to cook rather than just get fed.
