Where BetterHelp wins

BetterHelp matched us with a suitable therapist in 19 hours on average and offers live video, phone, chat and messaging in one plan. The counsellor-switching process was frictionless, which matters if the first match is wrong.

Where Talkspace wins

Talkspace accepts more insurance plans, including several Medicaid programs BetterHelp does not, and offers psychiatry alongside therapy. Its messaging-first model suits some people but the live-session allowance is stricter on lower tiers.

Our verdict

BetterHelp if you want the fastest match and flexible formats. Talkspace if insurance coverage or psychiatry is the deciding factor — check your plan before choosing either.

BetterHelp vs Talkspace FAQ

Which is better, BetterHelp or Talkspace?

BetterHelp matched us faster and includes more session formats. Talkspace accepts more insurance plans.

Does insurance cover either?

Sometimes. Talkspace accepts more plans including some Medicaid. Always verify your specific coverage directly.

How much do they cost per week?

Both start around $69-70 per week, comparable to in-network insurance copays for some plans.

Can I change therapists?

Yes on both platforms, and it is straightforward. BetterHelp was slightly smoother in our testing.
