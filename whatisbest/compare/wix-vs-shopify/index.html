Design: Wix vs Shopify

Wix's unstructured drag-and-drop editor is the most flexible in the category: you can place any element anywhere, create custom animations, and achieve designs Shopify themes can't match without Liquid coding. Shopify themes are polished but structured — you're designing within commerce templates, not from scratch.

Selling power: Wix vs Shopify

Shopify's checkout is a conversion machine: one-page flow, Shop Pay one-tap repeat purchases, and dynamic buttons for Apple Pay / Google Pay. Wix supports selling well but can't match Shopify's conversion optimization. Shopify also owns POS, inventory management, and 8,000+ apps — infrastructure Wix doesn't have.

Pricing: Wix vs Shopify

Both start at $29/month, but Shopify charges 0.5-2% extra on transactions unless you use Shopify Payments. Wix has no transaction fees on most plans. For high-volume sellers, Shopify's total cost of ownership is lower thanks to better conversion and shipping discounts. For starters, Wix is simpler to budget.

Our verdict

Shopify wins for stores where revenue is the primary goal. Wix wins for beautiful sites that happen to sell. If you plan to scale an e-commerce business, start on Shopify — migrating from Wix later is painful.

Wix vs Shopify FAQ

Is Wix better than Shopify for e-commerce?

Shopify for serious selling; Wix for design-first sites that also sell. Shopify's checkout alone converts ~15% better.

Which is cheaper, Wix or Shopify?

Wix is simpler (no transaction fees); Shopify at scale costs less thanks to better conversion and infrastructure.

Can I migrate from Wix to Shopify?

Yes, but it's manual. Products, customers, and orders import via CSV; page content needs rebuilding.

Which has better templates?

Wix — 900+ templates with more unique designs. Shopify themes are polished but commerce-focused.

Which is better for dropshipping?

Shopify — native DSers/Spocket integration and the dropshipping ecosystem lives on Shopify.
