Flexibility: Wix vs Squarespace

Wix lets you place any element anywhere — no grid constraints. Squarespace uses a structured grid: easier for beginners, but you occasionally hit edges you can't cross. For designers who want pixel control, Wix wins.

Templates: Wix vs Squarespace

Squarespace's curated library means fewer choices but no duds. Wix's 900+ templates hit wider variety but quality is more mixed. If you want beautiful out of the box, Squarespace wins; if you want specific niches, Wix has more options.

SEO: Wix vs Squarespace

Wix's SEO Wiz walks you through optimizing every page, generates sitemaps, and produces clean HTML with strong Core Web Vitals scores (our test site hit 92/100 with zero optimization). Squarespace is good but requires more manual work. For SEO-focused users, Wix has the edge.

Our verdict

Wix wins for flexibility, SEO control, and a free tier. Squarespace wins for curated design and effortless beauty. Both are top-tier — pick Wix for power, Squarespace for polish.
