Saatva's dual-coil system provides better edge support and spinal alignment, particularly for back and stomach sleepers over 150 lbs. Nectar's memory foam conforms more but can feel "stuck" for combo sleepers or heavier bodies.

Cooling: Saatva vs Nectar

Saatva sleeps significantly cooler — its coil construction allows 30% more airflow in our thermal tests. Nectar's gel-infused memory foam is good for a foam mattress, but can't match the breathability of coils.
