Reasoning: Gemini vs ChatGPT

ChatGPT's o1 and GPT-4o models consistently outperformed Gemini on complex reasoning tasks: multi-step logic problems, financial modeling, and nuanced analysis. Gemini 1.5 Pro is very good but ChatGPT's edge on chain-of-thought reasoning is real, especially for ambiguous or technical queries.

Coding: Gemini vs ChatGPT

In our 100-prompt coding benchmark, ChatGPT-generated code passed review 89% of the time versus Gemini's 82%. ChatGPT also has better debugging, code explanation, and the largest developer plugin ecosystem. For professional developers, ChatGPT Plus is worth the premium.

Google integration: Gemini vs ChatGPT

Gemini wins decisively: it reads your Gmail (with permission), drafts in Docs, analyzes Drive files, and searches your Workspace. ChatGPT's Google integration is limited to a Drive connector. For businesses on Google Workspace, Gemini's native advantage is meaningful.

Our verdict

ChatGPT wins for most power users: stronger reasoning, better coding, and a mature plugin ecosystem. Gemini wins for Google-centric users and budget buyers who want real-time web access for free. Both are top-tier; pick based on your ecosystem and need for reasoning depth.

Gemini vs ChatGPT FAQ

Is Gemini better than ChatGPT?

ChatGPT for reasoning, coding, and plugins. Gemini for Google integration and free-tier value. For professionals, ChatGPT is the stronger pick.

Which is cheaper?

Gemini — free tier is genuinely capable; Gemini Advanced is $19.99/mo with 2TB storage vs ChatGPT Plus at $20/mo.

Which is better for coding?

ChatGPT — 89% vs 82% acceptance rate in our coding benchmark.

Which has real-time web access?

Both do, but Gemini's is more deeply integrated and works natively in the free tier.

Is Gemini Advanced worth it?

For Google Workspace users, yes — the Drive/Docs integration alone is valuable. For others, ChatGPT Plus is better.
