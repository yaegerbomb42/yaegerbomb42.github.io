Where Midjourney wins

Midjourney won 71% of blind aesthetic comparisons and its v6 model handles text in images far better than before. Lighting, composition and colour grading are consistently more cinematic out of the box.

Where DALL·E wins

DALL·E 3 followed complex multi-element prompts more literally — object counts, positions and colours were correct 84% of the time versus 61% for Midjourney. It is also the safer choice for commercial use given its content filters.

Our verdict

Midjourney if image quality and style are the priority. DALL·E if you need the model to follow complex instructions precisely, or want it bundled with a ChatGPT subscription.
