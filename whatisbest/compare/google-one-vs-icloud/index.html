Where Google One wins

Google One costs $9.99/month for 2 TB versus $9.99 for iCloud+ at the same capacity, but includes sharing with up to five people and works identically on every platform. Its photo search and editing tools are also more capable.

Where iCloud+ wins

iCloud+ integrates perfectly with Apple hardware — Photos, device backups and Find My all work without thought, and Private Relay is a genuinely useful bonus. Outside Apple it is second-rate, with a clunky web interface and no Linux support.

Our verdict

Google One unless every device you own is an Apple product. iCloud+ if you are deep in the Apple ecosystem and device backups matter more than flexibility.

Google One vs iCloud+ FAQ

Is Google One better than iCloud+?

For anyone not exclusively on Apple hardware, yes — same price, better cross-platform support and stronger photo tools.

Which is cheaper for 2 TB?

Both charge $9.99/month for 2 TB, so the deciding factor is ecosystem rather than price.

Does Google One include a VPN?

Yes, on the 2 TB plan and above. iCloud+ includes Private Relay, which is more limited and Safari-only.

Can I share iCloud+ storage with family?

Yes, with up to five other people, matching Google One's family allowance.
