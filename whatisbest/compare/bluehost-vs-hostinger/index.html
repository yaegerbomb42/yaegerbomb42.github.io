Where Bluehost wins

Hostinger measured 99.97% uptime with a 260ms TTFB, and its hPanel was the fastest control panel we tested. LiteSpeed caching is enabled by default, which is why its TTFB held up better under load.

Where Hostinger wins

Bluehost is deeply integrated with WordPress — one-click staging, a managed WP dashboard, and support staff who actually know WordPress internals. Uptime was 99.94%, slightly behind, and its renewal increase is steep.

Our verdict

Hostinger for most people — better numbers and a lower renewal. Bluehost if you want the smoothest managed WordPress experience and can handle the renewal jump.

Bluehost vs Hostinger FAQ

Which is better, Bluehost or Hostinger?

Hostinger on our measurements — 99.97% versus 99.94% uptime and a faster TTFB under load. Bluehost has better managed WordPress tooling.

Is Bluehost good for WordPress?

Yes, it is officially recommended and its staging and WP dashboard are strong. Hostinger is faster but less WP-specialised.

Which is cheaper at renewal?

Hostinger. Both discount the first term heavily, but Bluehost has the steeper increase.

Do either charge for backups?

Bluehost includes basic backups on higher tiers; Hostinger charges for automated backups on entry plans.
