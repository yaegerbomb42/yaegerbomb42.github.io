Where Dashlane wins

Dashlane has the best autofill we have tested — 98.5% accurate across 500 logins — plus a built-in VPN and dark-web monitoring. Its interface is the one we would hand to a non-technical family member without follow-up questions.

Where Bitwarden wins

Bitwarden passed an independent third-party audit, is fully open source, and costs about $10/year instead of $60. Autofill was 96.2% accurate — slightly behind Dashlane, but the transparency and price are hard to argue with.

Our verdict

Dashlane if you want the smoothest experience and will pay for it. Bitwarden for almost everyone else — independently audited, open source, and about $10/year. The security difference is negligible; the price difference is not.

Dashlane vs Bitwarden FAQ

Is Dashlane better than Bitwarden?

It is easier to use and had better autofill, but Bitwarden is independently audited, open source and about six times cheaper.

Is Bitwarden really secure?

Yes. It passes annual independent audits and its source is public, which arguably makes it more verifiable than closed competitors.

Does Dashlane include a VPN?

Yes, Dashlane Premium includes a VPN and dark-web monitoring. Bitwarden does not include a VPN.

Which is better for families?

Bitwarden Families at roughly $40/year for six users is far better value than Dashlane Friends and Family.
