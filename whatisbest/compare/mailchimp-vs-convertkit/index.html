Where Mailchimp wins

ConvertKit built a working welcome sequence with tagging and segmentation in 25 minutes, and its visual automation builder is genuinely intuitive. Pricing scales with subscribers only, not contacts, which matters for growing lists.

Where ConvertKit wins

Mailchimp has far better templates and a broader toolset — landing pages, postcards, social ads — but its automations were clunkier and it charges for unsubscribed contacts, which inflated our bill by 18%.

Our verdict

ConvertKit for creators, newsletters and anyone building an audience. Mailchimp if you need landing pages, postcards and a broader marketing suite in one place.

Mailchimp vs ConvertKit FAQ

Is ConvertKit better than Mailchimp?

For creators and small lists, yes — better automations and it charges on subscribers rather than total contacts.

Why did my Mailchimp bill seem too high?

Mailchimp charges for unsubscribed and archived contacts, which inflated our test bill by 18%.

Which has better email templates?

Mailchimp, by a clear margin. ConvertKit templates are functional but plainer.

Which is cheaper at 5,000 subscribers?

ConvertKit, at roughly $29/month versus $50 for Mailchimp — about 42% less.
