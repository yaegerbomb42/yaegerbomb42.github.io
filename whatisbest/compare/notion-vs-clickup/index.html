Where Notion wins

Notion loads instantly and the block model means you build exactly the document you need. Docs, wikis, light databases and a CRM all work without configuring anything. ClickUp felt heavy by comparison — every interaction waited on a server round trip.

Where ClickUp wins

ClickUp ships Gantt charts, sprint management, workload views, time tracking and dependency mapping that Notion simply does not have. If you run a real software or delivery team, that depth justifies the learning curve.

Our verdict

Notion for most teams, especially where documentation plus light tracking is the real need. ClickUp if you need genuine project management depth and will invest the setup time. Do not pick ClickUp purely because it is cheaper — the setup cost is real.

Notion vs ClickUp FAQ

Is Notion better than ClickUp?

For most teams, yes — faster in daily use, quicker to learn and more adaptable. ClickUp only wins if you need built-in Gantt, sprints and workload management.

Which is cheaper, Notion or ClickUp?

ClickUp, at roughly $7 per user per month versus $10 for Notion on comparable paid tiers.

Can ClickUp replace Notion?

Partly. Its docs feature covers the basics, but our teams reached for Notion whenever a document mattered — the editing experience is materially better.

Which has the better free tier?

ClickUp, by a wide margin. Notion restricts guests and block history on the free plan; ClickUp allows unlimited members.
