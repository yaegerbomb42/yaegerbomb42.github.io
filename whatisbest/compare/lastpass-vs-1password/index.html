Security: 1Password vs LastPass

1Password's secret-key model is the strongest we've reviewed: two-factor encryption means even a full server breach exposes only useless encrypted vaults. LastPass has strengthened significantly since its 2022 incident, but 1Password's clean record gives it the trust advantage.

Apps: 1Password vs LastPass

1Password's apps are faster, more polished, and more consistent across platforms. LastPass has improved its UI but still feels a generation behind in speed and design. For families and teams, 1Password's sharing and permission controls are the best in the industry.

Price: 1Password vs LastPass

Both are around $3/month for individuals. LastPass has a real free tier; 1Password does not. For budget users, LastPass is the cheaper entry point; for those willing to pay for premium security, 1Password is worth the premium.

Our verdict

1Password wins for most users: stronger security, better apps, and smoother family sharing. LastPass wins if price is the driver and you want a free tier. Given the stakes (your entire digital identity), we recommend 1Password for anyone storing sensitive credentials.
