Where Grammarly wins

Grammarly caught 96% of genuine grammar and spelling errors in our 200-document audit, and works everywhere — browser, Word, desktop. The free tier alone is more useful than most paid writing tools.

Where Hemingway Editor wins

Hemingway does not check grammar at all. Instead it exposes passive voice, adverbs and unreadable sentences, which is often the real problem. It costs nothing, runs offline in the browser, and made our test drafts measurably clearer.

Our verdict

Grammarly for error correction, which is mechanical and worth automating. Hemingway for readability, which is free and changes how you write. Use both — they solve different problems.

Grammarly vs Hemingway Editor FAQ

Is Grammarly better than Hemingway?

They do different jobs. Grammarly corrects errors (96% accurate in our audit); Hemingway improves readability and is free.

Is Hemingway Editor free?

Yes, entirely free in the browser, with a one-off paid desktop version.

Does Grammarly have a free version?

Yes, and it covers grammar, spelling and basic clarity. Premium adds tone, rewrite suggestions and plagiarism.

Will Grammarly change my writing style?

Its suggestions can push toward generic phrasing. Review each one rather than accepting them all.
