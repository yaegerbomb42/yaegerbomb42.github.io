E-commerce power: Shopify vs Squarespace

Shopify's checkout is a conversion machine: one-page, Shop Pay, and dynamic buttons for Apple Pay / Google Pay. Squarespace's checkout is clean but can't match the conversion optimization. Shopify also has inventory management, shipping discounts, and native POS — everything a growing store needs.

Design: Shopify vs Squarespace

Squarespace templates are more beautiful and its editor is friendlier for designers. Shopify's theme store has solid options but lags behind Squarespace in visual polish. If aesthetics drive your brand, Squarespace wins; if conversion matters more, Shopify's themes are good enough.

Pricing: Shopify vs Squarespace

Squarespace's e-commerce plans start at $27/month; Shopify's start at $29/month. Shopify charges 0.5-2% transaction fees on non-Shopify Payments gateways; Squarespace charges 3% on its entry plan. At scale, Shopify's infrastructure is worth the premium.

Our verdict

Shopify wins for stores first and foremost. Squarespace wins for beautiful sites that also sell. For a small catalog, Squarespace is fine; for a growing business, choose Shopify.

Shopify vs Squarespace FAQ

Is Shopify better than Squarespace for e-commerce?

Yes — purpose-built for selling, with better checkout, apps, and multi-channel support.

Which is cheaper, Shopify or Squarespace?

Squarespace — $27/month vs $29/month entry-level.

Can Squarespace handle a growing store?

For small catalogs, yes. For growing stores, Shopify's infrastructure, apps, and checkout optimization are worth the extra cost.

Which has better templates?

Squarespace — more beautiful and curated. Shopify themes are functional but less polished.

Can I migrate from Squarespace to Shopify later?

Yes — product and customer data can be exported and imported. Rebuilding pages is the manual work. Migrating early is easier than migrating after growth.
