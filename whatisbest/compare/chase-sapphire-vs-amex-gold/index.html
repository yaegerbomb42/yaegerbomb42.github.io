Where Chase Sapphire Preferred wins

Chase Sapphire Preferred gave better value on travel — 5× on flights booked directly, a $50 hotel credit, and points transfer to more airline and hotel partners. Its $95 fee is also half of the Amex Gold.

Where Amex Gold wins

Amex Gold earns 4× on groceries and dining, which is the highest rate available in those categories, plus a $120 dining credit. If food is your largest expense it wins easily, but the $250 fee and category limits narrow its appeal.

Our verdict

Chase Sapphire Preferred for most people, especially anyone who travels. Amex Gold only if groceries and dining dominate your spending enough to absorb the $250 fee. Rewards rates and terms change frequently — verify before applying.
