How we test

Rankings you can trust are built on process, not vibes.

1 · Buy retail

We purchase every product with our own money at retail price. No review units, no special treatment.

2 · Test for weeks

Each product goes through a minimum 30-day hands-on test protocol with instrumented measurements, not first impressions.

3 · Score on data

Weighted scorecards across 6–10 criteria per category. Every score traces back to a number we measured.

4 · Re-test quarterly

Prices change, updates ship, quality slips. We re-verify rankings every 90 days and demote products that regress.

Common questions

Are your rankings sponsored?

No. Brands cannot pay for placement. Our revenue comes from affiliate commissions when you click out and buy — and those commissions are roughly similar across the top products in each category, so we have no financial reason to rank #2 over #1 or vice versa.

How do you make money?

Affiliate commissions. When you click a "Check price" link and make a purchase, the retailer pays us a small percentage. It costs you nothing extra — prices are identical whether you use our link or not.

How often are rankings updated?

Every category is fully re-tested quarterly, and pricing/availability is checked monthly. Each page shows its last verified date at the top.

What if I disagree with a ranking?

Weighting is personal. If budget matters more to you than performance, sort the comparison table by value score — the right pick for you might be our #3. We publish the full score breakdowns so you can re-weight for your priorities.
