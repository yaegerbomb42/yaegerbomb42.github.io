Why so many VPNs fail at streaming

Streaming services maintain large blocklists of known VPN IP ranges and aggressively rotate them. A VPN that worked last month can fail this month without changing anything on your side. This is why we re-test weekly instead of once — a single snapshot tells you almost nothing.

The services that win invest in IP rotation and dedicated streaming servers. The cheap ones reuse the same addresses until they are burned.

What actually matters for 4K

Bandwidth is rarely the constraint — any of these handles 4K easily. What causes buffering is latency and packet loss on the tunnel, plus the CDN hop after you exit. Server distance is the single biggest lever: connect to a server in the same country as the content library you want.

If you stream on a Fire Stick or smart TV, confirm the VPN has a native app for it. Manual WireGuard configuration on a TV is possible but fragile across firmware updates.
