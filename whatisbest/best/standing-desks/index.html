Standing desk FAQ

What is the best standing desk in 2026?

The Uplift V2 — most stable at full height (0.3° wobble), 355 lb capacity, flawless 5,000-cycle test, 15-year warranty. On a budget, the FlexiSpot E7 Pro gets within 15% for $170 less.

Are standing desks actually worth it?

For back-pain sufferers in our tester group, 4 of 5 reported reduced discomfort when alternating hourly. A wobble-prone desk will make you quit within a month — stability matters more than any other spec, which is why it's 35% of our score.

What size desktop should I get?

48×30" minimum for dual monitors; 60×30" if you keep speakers or a laptop docked alongside. Depth matters more than width — 30" lets your monitors sit at proper eye distance.

How much weight capacity do I need?

Dual monitors + arms + desktop: ~40 lb. Add a tower PC on the desk and you want 150 lb+ working load. All five picks handle 275 lb+, so it's a non-issue above the budget tier.
