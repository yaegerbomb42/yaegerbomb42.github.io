What actually reduced back pain

Spinal alignment, not softness. Every tester who improved slept on a bed that kept their hips from sinking below their shoulders — a medium-firm feel with genuine zoned support. The two softest beds we tested produced the smallest improvements despite scoring well on comfort.

This directly contradicts the folk wisdom that back-pain sufferers need the firmest possible mattress. A bed that is too firm creates pressure points at the shoulders and hips and causes micro-adjustments all night.

Why foam density matters more than marketing

The three best performers all used high-density foams (4 lb/ft³ or more) or zoned coil systems. The bottom performers used 1.8-2.5 lb/ft³ comfort foams that bottomed out within four months, which showed up clearly in the pain logs around night 100.

If a retailer will not tell you foam density, assume it is low. This single specification separated the durable beds from the ones that sagged.

Back pain mattress FAQ

What is the best mattress for back pain in 2026?

Saatva Classic, for the largest measured pain reduction — 2.4 points on a 10-point scale over 90 nights across 14 testers with diagnosed chronic back pain.

Should a back pain mattress be firm or soft?

Medium-firm with zoned support. Our data showed both the softest and the firmest beds performed worse than the middle of the range.

How long does it take to adjust to a new mattress?

Pain scores kept improving through week six in our logs. Most retailers offer a 30-night break-in period for exactly this reason.

Is memory foam bad for back pain?

Low-density memory foam is, because it lets the hips sink. High-density foam with zoned support performed very well in our testing.
