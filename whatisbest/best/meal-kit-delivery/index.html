Are meal kits healthier than takeout?

Generally yes — measured portions, fresh ingredients, and calorie transparency. Most services offer calorie-smart, keto, or high-protein filters.

Do meal kits reduce food waste?

Yes. Precise portions mean less unused produce rotting in the fridge. Packaging is the trade-off — look for services with recyclable insulation.
