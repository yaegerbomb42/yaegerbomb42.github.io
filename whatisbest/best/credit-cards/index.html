Credit card FAQ

What is the best credit card in 2026?

Chase Sapphire Preferred, with a 9.5/10 score. Best balance of rewards, bonus, and fee.

What credit score do I need?

Most top rewards cards require good-to-excellent credit (670+). Sapphire Preferred typically approves 700+, while Discover it and Citi Custom Cash are more accessible at 670+.

Are annual fees worth it?

Yes if perks exceed the fee. Sapphire Preferred's 60k bonus ($750) and strong earning cover the $95 fee at ~$3,800 in annual travel spend. Below that, no-fee cards win.

Should I get a travel card or cashback card?

Travel cards win if you fly 2+ times per year — points transfer to partners at outsized value. Cashback wins for simplicity and groceries/gas-heavy spenders. Many households run both.

Will applying hurt my credit?

A single application typically lowers your score by 5-10 points temporarily. If you're approved and use the card responsibly, it recovers and grows within months.
