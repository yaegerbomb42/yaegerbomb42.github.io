Mattress FAQ

What is the best mattress in 2026?

The Saatva Classic (Luxury Firm) for back and combination sleepers (9.4/10). Side sleepers on a budget should get the Nectar Premier; dedicated side sleepers with more to spend, the Helix Midnight Luxe.

What firmness should I buy?

Side sleepers: medium-soft to medium (5–6/10). Back sleepers: medium-firm (6–7/10). Stomach sleepers: firm (7–8/10). If you're over 230 lbs, go one step firmer than your position suggests.

How long should a $1,000+ mattress last?

7–10 years for hybrids like Saatva/Helix; 8+ for high-density foam. Sagging of 1"+ is the usual failure point — all five picks above warrant against it.

Is a mattress trial really risk-free?

Mostly. Nectar (365 nights) and Saatva (365 nights, minus $99 pickup fee) are the most generous. Always check return fees — they range from $0 to $99 across this list.
