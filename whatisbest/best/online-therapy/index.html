Online therapy FAQ

What is the best online therapy platform in 2026?

BetterHelp, with a 9.3/10 score. Largest network, fastest matching, and unlimited messaging.

Is online therapy covered by insurance?

Talkspace and Cerebral accept many plans. BetterHelp is out-of-pocket but offers financial aid. Check with your insurer — telehealth coverage has expanded.

Can online therapy prescribe medication?

No. Therapists provide talk therapy; psychiatrists prescribe. For medication, choose Cerebral, Brightside, or Talkspace's psychiatry tier.

How do I know if my therapist is a good fit?

Most platforms let you switch therapists anytime at no cost. Look for feeling heard, safe, and challenged — if those aren't present after 2-3 sessions, request a switch.

Is online therapy confidential?

Yes — all five platforms use encrypted, HIPAA-compliant systems. Check each platform's privacy policy for specifics on data handling and crisis protocols.
