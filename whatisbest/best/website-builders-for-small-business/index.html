What we measured

Time from empty account to a publishable five-page site, by two testers with no prior experience of the platform. Then Lighthouse mobile scores on the default template, and the true 12-month cost including the plan tier needed for the features a small business actually requires.

The gap between headline price and real cost was the biggest finding. Several builders advertise a low entry plan, then require a mid-tier plan the moment you want to remove ads, take payments, or connect a domain properly.

Speed to launch versus long-term control

Wix was quickest to something that looked finished, but produced the weakest mobile scores. Squarespace balanced speed and quality best. WordPress took more than twice as long to reach the same result but gave complete control and the lowest running cost.

For a small business where the owner has no developer, time-to-launch is usually worth more than theoretical flexibility. That trade-off reverses once traffic or product complexity grows.

Our verdict

Squarespace for most small businesses: best combination of speed, mobile performance and support. Shopify if you are primarily selling products and expect to scale past a few hundred SKUs. WordPress if annual cost matters most and someone technical is involved.
