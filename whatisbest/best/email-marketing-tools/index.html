Email marketing FAQ

What is the best email marketing tool in 2026?

Mailchimp, with a 9.4/10 score. Best deliverability (96.1%), most polished automation, and broadest feature set for most businesses.

What's the best free email marketing tool?

Mailchimp's free tier (500 contacts) is the most complete. ConvertKit's free tier is better for creators (1,000 subscribers), and Brevo's is best for larger lists (unlimited contacts).

Which has the best deliverability?

In our testing, Mailchimp (96.1%) and ActiveCampaign (95.4%) led. Deliverability depends heavily on your sender reputation, so results vary — but platform infrastructure matters.

Is Mailchimp worth paying for?

Yes, for most SMBs. The free tier is fine to start, but the automation, A/B testing, and predictive segmentation on paid plans deliver measurable ROI for growing lists.

Can I switch email tools later?

Yes — all major platforms let you export subscribers. Rebuilding automations is the main cost. Pick a platform that scales to avoid a painful migration.
