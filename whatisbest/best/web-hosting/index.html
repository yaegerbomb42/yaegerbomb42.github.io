Hosting FAQ

What is the best web hosting in 2026?

Hostinger for most people (9.4/10): 99.99% uptime, 385ms TTFB, from $2.99/mo. If support quality matters most, SiteGround is worth the premium.

How much should web hosting cost?

$3–8/month for shared hosting is the sweet spot in 2026. Anything under $2/mo cuts corners on server density; anything over $10/mo shared is overpaying unless it's managed WordPress.

What's the catch with $2.99 hosting deals?

Intro pricing. Every major host renews 2–4× higher. Buy the longest initial term (usually 48 months) to lock the rate, and check our renewal column before choosing.

Shared vs cloud hosting — which do I need?

Shared (Hostinger, SiteGround) covers sites up to ~50k monthly visits. Above that, or if you need server-level control, go cloud with Cloudways — you skip a painful migration later.
