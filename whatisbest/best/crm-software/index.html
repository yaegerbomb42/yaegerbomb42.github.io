CRM software FAQ

What is the best CRM in 2026?

HubSpot CRM, with a 9.4/10 score. Best free tier, easiest onboarding, and a platform that scales with you.

What's the best free CRM?

HubSpot's free CRM — up to 2,500 contacts, deal pipeline, email tracking, and meeting scheduling at no cost.

HubSpot vs Salesforce, which is better?

HubSpot for SMBs and speed to value; Salesforce for enterprise complexity and customization. Most teams should start with HubSpot.

Can small teams use a CRM?

Yes — modern CRMs are affordable and easy. Pipedrive, Zoho, and HubSpot free tiers give small teams enterprise-grade pipeline tracking without enterprise complexity.

How long does it take to set up a CRM?

Basic HubSpot or Pipedrive can be live in under an hour. Enterprise Salesforce deployments typically take weeks or months with consultants.
