What we actually measured

One uptime check per minute per host for 90 days — 129,600 checks — from four geographic regions, plus load testing at 50 and 500 concurrent users to find where TTFB degrades. Marketing claims were ignored entirely.

We also logged every support ticket verbatim: when we sent it, when a human replied, and whether the first reply actually answered the question.

The renewal price trap

This is the single biggest cost difference between hosts and the one most reviews omit. SiteGround and Hostinger discount the first term heavily, then renew at 2.4× to 3.1×. Cloudways, Kinsta and WP Engine keep pricing flat across terms.

If you plan to keep the site for years, the flat-pricing hosts are often cheaper in total despite a higher first invoice. Do the three-year maths before you commit.

Pros

  • Compare 3-year totals, not first-invoice price
  • Flat-pricing hosts: Cloudways, Kinsta, WP Engine
  • Migration is often free if you ask support

Cons

  • Intro discounts of 60-75% are not sustainable pricing
  • Some hosts charge for backups after term 1

Our verdict

SiteGround for most WordPress sites: best support responsiveness and uptime we measured. Cloudways if you want flat renewal pricing and the freedom to switch underlying cloud providers. Avoid shared hosts that oversell CPU — the TTFB difference at 500 concurrent users was stark.

WordPress hosting FAQ

What is the best WordPress hosting in 2026?

SiteGround for support speed and measured uptime. Cloudways if you want flat renewal pricing and provider flexibility.

How much should WordPress hosting cost?

Expect $3-15/month for good shared hosting, $25-100+ for managed or cloud hosting. Anything under $2/month is oversold shared capacity.

Do I need managed WordPress hosting?

Only if you run a business site where downtime costs money. Managed hosts handle updates, caching, staging and security, at roughly 3× the price.

Why did my renewal price jump so much?

Introductory discounts of 60-75% are standard and expire after the first term. Flat-pricing hosts like Cloudways and Kinsta avoid this entirely.
