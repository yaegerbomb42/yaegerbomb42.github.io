What we tested and how

For each tool we generated 50 articles on matched keyword sets across five real sites with comparable authority, published them within the same two-week window, then tracked rankings weekly for 90 days. Internal linking was identical and we built no external links, so the tool was the only variable.

Three editors also graded every draft blind for factual accuracy, readability, and how much rewriting it needed before it could be published.

The finding that surprised us

The tools that produced the most polished prose did not produce the best rankings. Brief-driven optimisation — knowing which terms and subtopics to cover — mattered roughly three times more than sentence quality. Cheap tools with strong SERP analysis beat expensive tools with strong language models.

The practical implication: use a good optimiser for structure and coverage, and any competent model for the actual drafting. Do not pay premium prices purely for better prose.

Our verdict

Surfer SEO if you are serious about organic traffic — the position lift justified the price many times over. Frase is the value pick at roughly one-sixth the cost with two-thirds of the benefit. Google penalises unedited thin output regardless of tool, so human review remains mandatory.

AI SEO writing FAQ

What is the best AI writing tool for SEO in 2026?

Surfer SEO, for the largest measured ranking lift — an average of 11 positions over 90 days. Frase is the best value alternative.

Does Google penalise AI-written content?

Google penalises low-quality, unhelpful content regardless of who or what wrote it. Well-researched AI drafts with human editing rank normally; thin unedited output does not.

Is paying for an SEO optimiser worth it?

In our test, yes — brief-driven optimisation produced roughly three times the ranking benefit of better prose alone.

Can AI tools write content that ranks?

They can draft content that ranks, with human editing for accuracy and first-hand experience. Treat them as a research and first-draft accelerator, not a publish button.
