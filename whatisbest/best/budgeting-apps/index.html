Budgeting FAQ

What is the best budgeting app in 2026?

Monarch Money (9.5/10) — 98% categorization accuracy, flawless sync, and the only app that combines budgets, investments and net worth in one place.

Is Monarch or YNAB better?

Monarch for most people: better automation, investments, and couples features. YNAB if you're fighting debt or living paycheck-to-paycheck — its zero-based method changes behavior more aggressively.

Are budgeting apps safe to connect to my bank?

Every app here uses read-only aggregator connections (Plaid/MX) with bank-level encryption. They can see balances but cannot move money. Enable app-level 2FA on top.

What happened to Mint?

Mint shut down in 2024. Monarch Money is the closest full-featured successor — and after 90 days, we think it's strictly better than Mint ever was.
