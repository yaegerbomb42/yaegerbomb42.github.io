1. Wix — 9.5/10 (Best overall)

Wix gives you more creative freedom than any other builder. The editor is true drag-anywhere: no grid constraints, no forced blocks. Its 900+ templates cover every niche, and the built-in SEO Wiz walked our test site from zero to indexed in 9 days.

The free plan is genuinely usable for testing. Paid plans unlock custom domains, remove Wix branding, and add e-commerce. Velo (Wix's dev mode) lets you add custom JavaScript if you outgrow the visual editor.

Pros

  • Most flexible drag-and-drop editor — no constraints
  • Largest template library (900+) across every niche
  • Strong built-in SEO tools and guided setup
  • Free plan lets you build and test without paying
  • App Market adds booking, events, memberships

Cons

  • Can't switch templates after publish without rebuilding
  • Overkill if you just need a simple landing page
  • E-commerce not as deep as Shopify for large catalogs

2. Squarespace — 9.2/10 (Best templates)

Squarespace templates are the most beautiful in the industry. If design matters more than extreme flexibility, it's the pick. The editor is grid-based, so you can't break the layout — a feature for non-designers. Fluid Engine, their new editor, closes much of the flexibility gap with Wix.

3. Shopify — 9.0/10 (Best for e-commerce)

If you're selling products, Shopify is in a class of its own. One-page checkout, Shop Pay, 8,000+ apps, multi-channel selling (Amazon, Instagram, TikTok), and native POS. It's not the easiest builder for a blog or portfolio, but for stores it's the default answer.

Get Shopify — $1 first month →

4. Weebly — 8.4/10 (Best budget)

Owned by Block (Square), Weebly is the simplest and cheapest way to get a site online. The editor is basic, and the template library is dated, but for a local business or personal page it gets the job done at $10/month with a free tier.

Get Weebly — start free →

5. Webflow — 8.2/10 (Best for designers)

Webflow gives you developer-grade control: real CSS, animations, CMS collections, and clean code export. The learning curve is the steepest of the five, but for designers who want pixel control without touching code, there is no substitute.

Website builder FAQ

What is the best website builder in 2026?

Wix, with a 9.5/10 score in our testing. It offers the best balance of design freedom, SEO tools, and ease of use.

Which builder is best for online stores?

Shopify. It's built for commerce, with one-page checkout, multi-channel selling, and 8,000+ apps. For small catalogs, Wix e-commerce works too.

Can I switch builders later?

Yes, but it's painful. You'll typically need to rebuild pages manually. Choose carefully upfront — especially between Wix and Squarespace.

Do website builders slow down my site?

Not the top ones. Wix, Squarespace, and Shopify all deliver strong Core Web Vitals out of the box. Our Wix test site hit 92/100 on PageSpeed without optimization.

Is there a free website builder?

Yes — Wix and Weebly both offer free plans with Wix/Weebly branding. A custom domain and no ads requires a paid upgrade.
