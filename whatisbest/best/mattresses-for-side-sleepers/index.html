The two numbers that decide it

Peak shoulder pressure and whether the spine stays neutral. If shoulder pressure is high you wake with numbness and pain. If the bed is too soft to keep the spine straight, you wake with lower back pain instead. You need both right, which eliminates most beds.

We mapped pressure at hours 2, 4 and 6 of sleep because foam behaviour changes as it warms to body temperature. A bed that felt supportive at minute five often did not by hour four.

Why side sleepers need more give

A side sleeper has roughly 40% less contact area with the mattress than a back sleeper, so the same body weight concentrates into far fewer square inches. This is why side sleepers consistently need a slightly softer feel than back sleepers on the same mattress.

The failure mode to watch for is hip sink: the mattress lets the hip drop, twisting the lumbar spine out of alignment for hours. Zoned or hybrid construction fixes this; uniform soft foam does not.

Our verdict

Nectar Classic is the pick — lowest shoulder pressure, neutral spine, and a 365-night trial at a mid-range price. Casper Wave if you want the most adjustable feel and can spend considerably more. If you wake with a numb arm, shoulder pressure is your problem, not overall firmness.

Side sleeper mattress FAQ

What is the best mattress for side sleepers in 2026?

Nectar Classic, for the lowest shoulder pressure we measured — 31% below the group average — while keeping the spine neutral.

Should side sleepers use a soft or firm mattress?

Slightly softer than back sleepers need, but with enough support to stop the hip sinking. Medium-firm with zoned support is the sweet spot.

Why do my shoulders go numb at night?

Peak pressure at the shoulder restricting blood flow. You need more give in the shoulder zone — exactly the problem a too-firm mattress creates.

How thick should a side sleeper mattress be?

12 inches or more generally. Thinner beds lack the comfort layer depth needed to cushion the shoulder and hip properly.
