VPN FAQ

What is the best VPN in 2026?

NordVPN, based on 90 days of instrumented testing: 94% speed retention, perfect streaming unblocking, and four independent no-log audits. It scores 9.6/10 on our weighted scorecard.

What's the cheapest VPN that's actually good?

Surfshark at ~$2.19/month on a two-year plan. It retains 86% of base speed and allows unlimited devices — the best cost-per-device in the industry.

Will a VPN slow down my internet?

Yes, marginally — the best services cost you 5-15% of raw speed. NordVPN lost only 6% in our tests. Avoid free VPNs, which often cut speeds by 50%+ and may monetize your data.

Can I use a VPN for Netflix?

Yes, and all five picks above unblock Netflix in our weekly tests. NordVPN and ExpressVPN have the longest streak of never failing a region switch.

Do I need a VPN at home?

If you want to hide traffic from your ISP (which can legally sell browsing data in the US), yes. It also prevents throttling of streaming/torrenting and protects on smart-TV and IoT devices via router installs.
