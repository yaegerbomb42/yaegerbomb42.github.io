2. Windscribe Free — 8.4/10 (Best data allowance)

10 GB per month once you confirm an email address, 11 server locations, and the strongest free-tier ad blocking we tested — R.O.B.E.R.T. blocks whole domain categories rather than a static list. Speeds were the best of any free tier at 81% retention.

The trade-off is logging: Windscribe records bandwidth totals per account to enforce the cap. That is defensible, but it is not the zero-knowledge posture Proton offers, and Canada is a Five Eyes jurisdiction.

3. Cloudflare WARP — 8.0/10 (Fastest, but it logs)

WARP is not strictly a VPN and we want to be precise: it encrypts your DNS and traffic, but Cloudflare can still see it and retains some logs for 24 hours. If your goal is hiding traffic from your ISP, it works brilliantly and kept 93% of base speed — the highest we measured.

If your goal is anonymity, it fails. You have simply moved your trust from your ISP to Cloudflare. Treat it as privacy hygiene, not a VPN.

Free VPN FAQ

What is the best free VPN in 2026?

Proton VPN Free. It is the only free VPN with unlimited data, no throttling, no ads, an audited no-logs policy and open-source apps. Nothing else comes close.

Are free VPNs safe to use?

Most are not. Free VPNs must make money somehow, and the usual model is logging and reselling browsing data or injecting ads. Stick to services with a real paid business and an audited policy.

Which free VPN gives the most data?

Proton VPN Free and Cloudflare WARP both offer unlimited data. Windscribe and Hide.me give 10 GB/month; TunnelBear only 2 GB/month.

Will a free VPN work with Netflix?

No. Every free tier we tested was blocked. Streaming needs a paid VPN with rotating IP pools.

How much speed do free VPNs cost you?

Between 7% and 30% of base speed in our tests. Cloudflare WARP was fastest at 93% retained; TunnelBear slowest at 70%.
