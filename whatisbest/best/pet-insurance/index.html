Pet insurance FAQ

What is the best pet insurance in 2026?

Embrace, with a 9.4/10 score. Best coverage, fast payouts, and a diminishing deductible.

Does pet insurance cover pre-existing conditions?

No — all insurers exclude conditions that existed before enrollment or waiting periods ended. This is why insuring young, healthy pets is so important.

Is pet insurance worth it for a healthy pet?

Yes. A single emergency surgery costs $3,000-$5,000+. Lock in coverage before conditions develop — once diagnosed, they become pre-existing exclusions.

Can I use any vet?

Yes — all five picks let you visit any licensed vet, specialist, or emergency clinic. Most reimburse you after you pay; Pets Best offers direct-pay options.

What about wellness coverage?

Embrace, Pets Best, and Spot offer wellness add-ons for vaccines, dental cleanings, and annual exams. At $10-$30/month, they're worth it if you budget for routine care anyway.
