Antivirus FAQ

What is the best antivirus in 2026?

Bitdefender, with a 9.5/10 score. Best detection (99.7%), lowest system impact, and reliable ransomware rollback.

Is Windows Defender enough?

For careful users, yes — Microsoft's built-in solution has improved dramatically. Paid suites add ransomware rollback, banking protection, VPN, and dark-web monitoring.

Can I run two antivirus programs?

No. They'll conflict and slow your system. Pick one real-time suite; supplement with periodic on-demand scans from a second engine like Malwarebytes Free if you want extra assurance.

Does antivirus protect against phishing?

Partially. Most suites include anti-phishing URL blocking, but vigilance is still essential. Bitdefender and Norton had the best phishing-block rates in our tests.

How often should I scan?

Real-time protection covers most threats. Run a quick scan weekly and a full scan monthly. Scheduled scans should run during idle time to avoid slowdowns.
