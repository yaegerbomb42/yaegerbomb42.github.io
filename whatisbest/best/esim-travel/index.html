eSIM FAQ

What is the best eSIM for travel in 2026?

Airalo, with a 9.4/10 score. Cheapest data rates, 200+ country coverage, and instant activation.

Do all phones support eSIM?

Most modern smartphones do: iPhone XS and later, Google Pixel 3 and later, and recent Samsung Galaxy S models. Check your phone's settings for an "Add eSIM" option.

Can I use my phone number with an eSIM?

Yes — use dual SIM mode: keep your primary line active for calls/texts, use the travel eSIM for data. Turn off data roaming on your home line.

Is an eSIM faster than carrier roaming?

Usually comparable, since eSIMs roam onto the same local towers. Airalo's local carrier partnerships often gave us faster 5G than our U.S. carrier's roaming profile.

Can I top up an eSIM?

Yes — all five picks let you buy more data instantly in-app. Keepgo's plans remain valid for 12 months, making them ideal for frequent travelers.
