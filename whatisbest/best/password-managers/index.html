Password manager FAQ

What is the best password manager in 2026?

1Password, with a 9.5/10 score. Best security architecture, polished apps, and smoothest passkey support.

What's the best free password manager?

Bitwarden. Open-source, audited, unlimited devices, and zero-knowledge encryption — all free.

What if the password manager company gets hacked?

Zero-knowledge architecture means the company never holds your encryption keys. Even a breach of their servers exposes only encrypted vaults that require your master password to unlock.

Should I store 2FA codes in my password manager?

Yes, for convenience — it enables autofill of codes. For maximum security, keep 2FA in a separate app so an attacker who compromises your vault doesn't get both factors.

Can I share passwords with family?

All five picks support secure sharing. 1Password and Bitwarden handle family plans best, with private and shared vaults per member.
