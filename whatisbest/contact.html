Contact

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The fastest way to reach us is email. We read everything and reply to most things, though we cannot answer individual product-recommendation requests in detail — our rankings are the long-form version of that answer.

What to send us

  • Corrections — a wrong price, a discontinued product, a figure that looks off. Include the page URL and what you believe is correct. These get priority.
  • Testing suggestions — a category we should cover, or a product we missed that you think deserves a place in an existing test.
  • Press and partnerships — media enquiries and legitimate business proposals.
  • Privacy and data requests — access, correction or deletion requests relating to our Privacy Policy.
  • Legal notices — anything relating to our Terms of Use or intellectual property.

Response times

Corrections are usually verified and applied within a few working days. General enquiries may take longer, particularly when we are mid-test on a category. If your message is urgent, say so in the subject line.

What we cannot help with

  • Personalised buying advice. Read the relevant ranking and its value-sorted table instead — that is what the scoring is for.
  • Technical support for products you bought elsewhere. Contact the vendor directly; they can actually fix it.
  • Guest posts, paid link placements or sponsored content of any kind.

Post

We operate as a small independent publication and prefer email for everything. Please do not send physical product samples — we buy our own test units at retail, and unsolicited units are returned or destroyed unreviewed.
