Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information WhatIsBest.cloud ("we", "us", "the site") collects when you visit, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We aim to keep this readable rather than legally decorative — if anything here is unclear, contact us.

The short version

  • We do not require an account, and we never ask for your name, address or payment details.
  • We use privacy-respecting analytics to count page views and referrals. We do not build advertising profiles of visitors.
  • Affiliate partners and our advertising network set their own cookies when you click out or load a page. We do not control those.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly

Every page on this site can be read without submitting anything. If you choose to use the optional voting feature, we store a randomly generated session identifier in your browser's local storage together with the choice you made. That identifier is not linked to your name, email address or IP address, and exists only so you are not counted twice and so your XP and streak persist between visits.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply.

Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, our server logs record technical data: IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, referring URL and timestamp. Logs are retained for a limited period for security and abuse investigation, then deleted. A web server cannot serve a page without recording this.

We use a first-party analytics script to measure aggregate page views, referrers and outbound click counts. It is configured without cross-site tracking or advertising identifiers.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. We use them sparingly:

  • Essential — a session identifier for the voting feature, held in local storage rather than a cookie.
  • Analytics — the aggregate measurement described above.
  • Third-party — affiliate networks and our advertising partner set their own cookies when their scripts load or when you click an outbound link. We do not receive the underlying data; the vendor does.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking third-party cookies does not affect your ability to read any page on this site.

Affiliate links and advertising

WhatIsBest is funded by affiliate commissions and advertising. When you click a "Check price" or "Get deal" link, the destination site learns that you arrived from WhatIsBest and may set a cookie to attribute a purchase to us. Outbound affiliate links are marked rel="sponsored nofollow" in line with FTC guidance and search engine requirements.

Our advertising partner may display banner and interstitial ads. That partner operates under its own privacy policy, which governs its data handling. We do not pass personal data to advertisers.

See our Affiliate Disclosure for how this affects — and does not affect — our rankings.

Legal bases (UK/EU visitors)

Where the GDPR applies, we process data on the following bases: legitimate interests for server logs and the aggregate analytics necessary to operate and secure the site; your consent, where required by law, for non-essential cookies; and contract necessity for anything you explicitly ask us to do, such as replying to an email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold almost no personal data — no accounts, no purchase records — requests are usually simple. Email us via the contact page and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Data retention

Server logs are kept for a limited operational period and then deleted. Aggregate analytics contains no personal identifiers and is retained indefinitely only in aggregate form. Email correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

Our rankings link to retailers, vendors and service providers. Once you leave WhatIsBest, that company's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the content or practices of sites we link to.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We keep dependencies updated and follow least-privilege practices, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be noted here rather than applied silently.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent via our contact page.
