    ❓ How to Play

    ⛏️ Mine. Click the sphere to harvest crystals. Rapid clicks build a combo multiplier — chain clicks to multiply your yield.

    ☄️ Comets. Golden comets occasionally fly past. Click one while it is visible for a big crystal jackpot.

    🛒 Upgrade. Buy Mining Lasers (per click), Auto-Miner drones (per second), and Charge Cores (global % bonus). Themes just look gorgeous.

    🧑‍🚀 Explorer. Track your stats, unlock 18 achievements, and read your journey log.

    🎯 Daily missions. Three objectives refresh every day — claim their rewards in the bar at the bottom.

    🌙 Offline. Your drones keep working while you are away (up to 8h, at half efficiency).

    💾 Save. Everything is stored in your browser (localStorage). Use the Explorer → Data panel to back up or transfer your save.
